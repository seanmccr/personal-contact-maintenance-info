Personal use information

Personal Contact Maintenance is operated solely for Sean McCrone’s own personal contact administration. There is no public account, subscription, payment or hosted contact service.

Audits are read-only. Proposed contact updates are subject to separate owner approval and fresh verification. Reports and equal contact counts do not guarantee that every provider contains identical information.

The owner retains control of connected account permissions and backup files. Availability depends on provider access, valid credentials and the owner’s Mac and scheduled-task environment being available.

This page describes the tool’s use; it does not create a service contract with outside users.
