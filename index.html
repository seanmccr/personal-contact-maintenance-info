Personal Contact Maintenance

Personal Contact Maintenance is a private tool operated by Sean McCrone to audit his own personal address books and prepare reviewed reconciliation.

The Google reader accesses the owner’s contacts in read-only mode and verifies the signed-in account. It stores a renewal credential in the Mac Keychain and creates private contact snapshots on the owner’s Mac. Weekly checks identify changes; monthly reviews can propose individual updates to the owner’s personal Outlook contacts.

Contact updates require separate owner review. The tool does not call, message, email or invite contacts, delete records or automatically merge identities. Employer accounts are excluded.

This application has no public registration or sign-in service. Its public information pages do not provide access to the owner’s address books.

For questions, use the support email displayed on the Google consent screen.
